# The Cabin copy

Read the current playable-story bible, plotline and nearby authored prose before writing. Public copy should carry dread through concrete place, implication and enclosure. Avoid generic indie-game sales language, lore dumps and jokey distance.

Dread does not require every sentence to be cold or terse. Ordinary comfort, warmth and credible routine give the wrongness something to disturb. Preserve the scene's register and the distinction between public copy and in-game second-person fiction. Do not invent mechanics or flatten deliberately strange prose to satisfy a style checklist.
