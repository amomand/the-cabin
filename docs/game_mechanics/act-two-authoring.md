# Act II authoring decisions (#264, Phase 3)

Elli answers the impossible with a job she can finish. The repair succeeds;
the image comparison leaves a different problem. The tree is fifty metres in,
and inspecting its roots is the next small, reasonable task she gives herself.

| Beat | Trigger | History / repeat | Later dependency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fox tracks | First morning arrival at the grounds; attention there also supports a loaded arrival | FOX_TRACKS once; later attention remembers without walking the prints again | Her avoidance before the camera; the forest's three tells |
| Test camera | Use northern camera at the grounds in the morning | camera_stage: untouched -> tested; no repair on looking | Full but ineffective battery and implausibly cold casing |

| Hare | First arrival among the dead pines; attention supports legacy saves | HARE once; returns stay on her boot marks, without re-encountering it | Deliberate echo in the encounter and night breathing |
| Missing deer path | First arrival in old woods; attention supports legacy saves | STONE_FORMATIONS retained as save identifier | Turning back triggers encounter only after the camera and all three specific tells |

The three tells are encountered on the walk, not hidden behind a sequence of
look commands. Rendering never writes history. Movement/attention share each
beat, so a load or repeated room redraw cannot replay it. Backtracking before
the old-woods encounter is allowed; fox and hare callbacks concern memory and
her own route, not fresh sightings. The encounter and false-cabin plot remain.

The site plan uses existing room ids. The main grounds lead north to the treeline
and west to the lake; the shore loop rejoins the same gated treeline. Dead Pines
lies between the treeline and Old Woods. The konttori has only its main-room door.
The wrong-layer southward walk keeps its separate exits.

Morning looks and listens preserve authored landscape and silence even when the
intent parser supplies contrary flavour. Waiting at the missing path retains
the impulse to turn back. Outdoor throws never conjure an answering knock; the
wrong woods remain indifferent.

seed and a readable waking-to-encounter route. Phases 4/5 plus remaining Phase 6
work follow only after Alex's playtest/read; Phase 7 is the final whole-story pass.
